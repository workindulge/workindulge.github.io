How Gameplay Works

Strike Iconic.AI uses your webcam to compare your pose with the reference pose in real time.

👁
STEP 01

Level Starts

The game shows a hint and hides the person's identity and photo. A preparation countdown starts. Press Space to skip the intro and preparation and start guessing immediately.

📷
STEP 02

Guess the Pose

The webcam detects your body pose and hand shape. Your pose is compared with the reference pose. The game calculates a similarity score and smooths it over recent frames.

🤸
STEP 03

Match the Pose

Move your body and hands until your pose reaches the configured match threshold. Once your pose matches, the hold timer begins.

⏱
STEP 04

Hold the Pose

Maintain the correct pose for the required hold time. If your similarity drops below the hold break threshold, the hold timer resets.

🏆
STEP 05

Pass the Level

Successfully hold the pose long enough and you PASS. Earn points and build your streak to increase your score.

🔄
STEP 06

Keep Playing

If the timer reaches zero before you complete the pose, you FAIL. A failed level gives 0 points and resets your streak to 0.
