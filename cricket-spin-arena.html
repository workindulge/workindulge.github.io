How To Play

STEP 01

Add Players

Enter the player names on the start screen and tap Start Game.

STEP 02

Spin the Wheel

Spin the cricket wheel and see whether you land on Dot, 1, 2, 4, 6, Wide, No Ball, Not Out or Wicket.

STEP 03

Score = Question

If you land on a scoring outcome such as 1, 2, 4 or 6, a cricket trivia question appears.

STEP 04

Guess One Letter

Type a single character that you think appears in the answer and hit Guess.

STEP 05

Get It Right

Correct guess? The letter is revealed, you earn the runs and spin again.

STEP 06

Keep Playing

Wrong guess means no runs and the turn passes to the next player. Keep going until the answer is completely revealed.

Special Outcomes

🏏

Wide / No Ball

Get an automatic run and spin the wheel again.

🔄

Not Out

Enjoy a free re-spin without losing your turn.

❌

Your turn ends immediately. No question or guess.

❓

✅

❌

Wrong Guess
